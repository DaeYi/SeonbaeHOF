Seonbae Hall of Fame

In honor of those who walked the path before us.

선배 — seonbae — is the Korean word for the elders and predecessors whose example you inherit. This hall gathers the lives of Korean Americans who paved the way, across a century and a half, so that the next generation can know the path they walk on and decide where to take it next.

Our Seonbae

선배 seonbae

The Korean honorific for the elders and predecessors who walked the path before you. The word carries reverence built into the language itself — a thing you owe rather than a thing you bestow.

The Seonbae Hall of Fame honors Korean Americans who shaped the community we inherit. Every figure here has passed away; their stories remain.

A proof-of-concept project. View on GitHub.
