선배 seonbae

The Korean honorific for the elders and predecessors who walked the path before you.

The Seonbae Hall of Fame honors Korean Americans who shaped the community we inherit. Every figure here has passed away; their stories remain.

A NetKAL × CKA project

This Hall of Fame was created by participants of the NetKAL fellowship — a leadership program of the Council of Korean Americans.
