## Notes

- **Group/family photos:** Two images (Helen Ahn, Ilhan New) are family/group photos rather than solo portraits. The site UI captions them appropriately. No solo Commons portraits exist for these two subjects.
- **Image dimensions vary widely** — from 107×174 (Park Yong-man) to 1483×2238 (Ilhan New). The Park Yong-man image in particular may look low-resolution when displayed; a higher-quality scan would improve presentation.
- **Other Seonbae:** 15 of the 25 figures in this collection do not have safely hotlinkable or downloadable public-domain images available. The website renders a placeholder for those.
